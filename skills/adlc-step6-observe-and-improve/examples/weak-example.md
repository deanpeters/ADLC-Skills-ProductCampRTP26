# Weak Example: Observation Anti-Pattern

Synthetic anti-pattern. All details are invented for teaching.

## Artifact

The pilot went well. Users like the agent and it saved time. We should expand it to all support queues and let it assign owners directly.

## Why It Passes

It is upbeat, action-oriented, and tied to apparent user value.

## Why It Fails

It cites no operational evidence, ignores traces and incidents, proposes an authority adjustment without proof, and offers only expansion instead of continue, change, reduce, rollback, retire, or reframe.

## Repair

Review traces, incidents, feedback, and outcomes; map learning to lifecycle artifacts; then decide whether authority should change.

## Retirement Variant

Synthetic anti-pattern. All details are invented for teaching.

### Artifact

Nobody uses the agent much anymore. We will switch it off at the end of the month.

### Why It Passes

It is decisive and saves running cost.

### Why It Fails

It gives no evidence for low use, names no successor or fallback, maps no dependents, sets no notice, and says nothing about credentials, stored data, or what to keep for the next agent.

### Repair

Confirm the reason with usage evidence, choose migrate or retire, map dependents from logs, stage notice through shutdown with a fallback live at notice, and add a shutdown checklist for credentials, data, and preserved evaluation sets.
