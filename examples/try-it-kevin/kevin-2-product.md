# Larkspur Club: the product's numbers now

Synthetic use case. All details are invented for teaching. Larkspur & Pine, the Larkspur Club, its people, and its figures are fictional.

## What the product's numbers say now

- Club membership: **flat** for three quarters (+1 percent).
- Margin per member: **down**, mostly from discounts and credits.
- Two competitors launched cheaper clubs this year.
- Leadership is debating **defend or reinvent**: protect the Club on retention and cost, or add a home-goods rental tier.
- There is no end-of-life plan for the Club, and Kevin has no retirement trigger.
