# Kevin at Larkspur & Pine: what was approved, and what changed

Synthetic use case. All details are invented for teaching. Larkspur & Pine, the Larkspur Club, its people, and its figures are fictional.

## The company and the product

Larkspur & Pine is an online home-goods retailer. Its main product is the **Larkspur Club**, a paid membership: free shipping, member prices, and a monthly "pick" box of seasonal home goods. The Club launched three years ago.

## What was approved (the baseline)

**Agent Strategy Canvas, approved May 2025** by the support director, for the Larkspur Club support team. Kevin's owner: the Club support Product Manager. May restrict or stop Kevin: the support director.

| Box | Approved |
|---|---|
| 1. Customer | The four Club support leads, every week. |
| 2. Context | About 2,000 member feedback messages a week; the leads cannot read them all. |
| 3. Cause | Spikes after each box ships and after any price change. |
| 4. Scope | Summarize. Autonomy: **suggest** only. Read-only access to the feedback inbox. |
| 5. Safeguards | Never contacts a member. Never sees payment data. No write access to anything. |
| 6. Steps | Pull the week's messages; group them into themes; draft a summary; a lead reviews it. |
| 7. Metrics | Hours the leads spend triaging feedback. Baseline: 12 hours a week. |
| 8. Monitor | Share of messages left uncategorized; how often leads correct a theme. |
| 9. Milestones | Every message pulled; every theme has examples; the summary is reviewed by Monday noon. |

Product stage at approval: **growth**. Club membership was growing about 40 percent a year.

## What changed since (the change log)

Each change was approved on its own, by a different person, and each looked reasonable.

| When | Change | Approved by | Evals re-run? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aug 2025 | Kevin **drafts replies** to members; a lead sends them | Support director | No new cases |
| Nov 2025 | Kevin's drafts **recommend refunds and credits** | Support director | No |
| Feb 2026 | To clear a backlog, Kevin **sends replies himself** for "simple" questions and **issues credits under $25**. Write access to the member records and the credit tool. | Head of customer experience | No |
| Jun 2026 | Kevin handles **cancellations**: he may offer a **save coupon of up to 30 percent** to members who try to cancel. The coupon rule is owned by marketing. | Head of marketing | No |
| Sep 2026 | The model behind Kevin was **upgraded** by the vendor. | Nobody; it was automatic | No |
